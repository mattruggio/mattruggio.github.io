Hey — I'm Matt.

I'm a software engineer who grew up on dial-up internet, Saturday morning cartoons, and staying up too late listening to grunge, hip-hop, and metal on the radio. I never really stopped being that kid.

This blog is where I put my tech thoughts out into the world: things I've learned, things I'm building, and the occasional detour into whatever's living rent-free in my head — music, movies, pop culture, or the way a particular riff from 1993 still hits different.

**What you'll find here:**

- Deep dives on software engineering concepts
- Notes from the trenches of building real things
- Opinions on tools, languages, and practices
- The occasional post that has nothing to do with code at all

---

I spend most of my professional life writing Ruby and thinking about clean, maintainable systems. I like open source, good documentation, and code that reads like it was written by someone who cared.

If you want to reach out, find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/mattruggio).
