**Multiplayer. Retro. Ruthless.**

RetroLive is a browser-based NBA manager sim built around the feel of crowding
around a single PS2 and burning a whole weekend on *NBA Live 2005* — running
franchises, simming seasons, and trash talking after every blown lead.

You and your league pick a roster year, run a fantasy draft, play out an 82-game
schedule, and fight through a playoff bracket — all at your own pace.

## What if…?

It isn't locked to one season. Rosters from **1996–97**, **2004–05**, and
**2025–26** all run through the same 2004-style sim engine, so the cross-era
questions get to actually play out. What if a prime Jordan had to draft against
today's league? What if 2026 Jokić dropped into the Iverson era?

More seasons are on the way.
