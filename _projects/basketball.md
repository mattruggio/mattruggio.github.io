This one is a love letter to both domain modeling and franchise-mode video games. It
models players, general managers, draft strategy, season generation, season simulation,
playoff generation, and playoff simulation.

It's the largest sandbox I have for exploring how far you can push rich domain objects
before you need a framework to hold them together.
