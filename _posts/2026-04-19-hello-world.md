Every blog starts somewhere.

Mine starts here — on a GitHub Pages site powered by Jekyll, styled to look like the terminal I spend most of my day staring at. Monospaced fonts, dark background, green text. If it gives you flashbacks to AOL Instant Messenger or a DOS prompt, mission accomplished.

## Why bother?

A few reasons:

1. **Writing forces clarity.** The best way to know if you actually understand something is to try explaining it to someone else. This blog is me doing that, in public, on the internet — where I'm definitely accountable now.

2. **The internet is better with more personal sites.** Big platforms come and go. A simple static site with plain HTML and CSS can outlive all of them.

3. **I have things to say.** About Ruby, about software design, about the bizarre beauty of a well-named function. About the fact that *Enter Sandman* was released 35 years ago and still sounds massive.

## What's coming

Mostly tech. Patterns I keep reaching for. Libraries I like. Ideas I'm chewing on.

But also: the weird intersection between the music and movies I grew up with and the way I think about building software. There's more overlap than you'd expect.

Anyway — hello. Glad you're here.
