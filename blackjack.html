House rules

  • Cards come from a six-deck shoe, the box a dealer feeds from, reshuffled once it drops below a deck.
  • Dealer stands on all 17, soft ones included.
  • Blackjack pays 3 to 2. Blackjack against blackjack is a push.
  • The dealer peeks for blackjack when the upcard can make 21.
  • Double on the first two cards only: one card, then the hand stands.
  • No splitting, no insurance, no surrender.

Your chips live in this browser and nowhere else. Nothing is sent anywhere, and clearing site data starts you over at 100.
